About Me

Ever since class 11th, I've been drawn toward a singular pursuit: teaching machines to think. What began as curiosity about artificial intelligence soon hardened into conviction — I chose Computer Science with a specialization in AI & ML for my B.Tech, and never looked back. Once I arrived at college, I refused to wait for coursework to hand me a starting point. I took my holidays into my own hands, began learning independently, and started committing every line of code to GitHub — transforming that early spark into a lasting discipline.

That self-driven rigor evolved into a structured 50-day roadmap spanning Python foundations, NumPy, Pandas, and the fundamentals of machine learning, sustained by daily problem-solving practice. I've since ventured beyond the syllabus too — engineering an IoT-based electricity theft detection system, and designing a hands-on Python curriculum for local students in Sunam. My ambition is to grow into a proficient AI/ML engineer, and every project I undertake is a deliberate step toward proving that early curiosity was worth chasing.
